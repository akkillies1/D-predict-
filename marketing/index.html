How it works

Discover → Research → Predict → Validate → Monitor

The workflow is intentionally sequential. A prediction is not treated as a trade simply because a model produced one.

01 · DiscoverSearch for a symbol and activate it.
02 · ResearchInspect market history and context.
03 · PredictGenerate model outputs and timing estimates.
04 · ValidateCheck temporal out-of-sample evidence and uncertainty.
05 · MonitorContinue observing the thesis instead of assuming success.

Built for evidence

No accuracy theatre.

D-Predict is being built around reproducible research, point-in-time data and explicit uncertainty. The system does not claim model accuracy until real historical data has been downloaded and evaluated.

Temporal evaluation

Models are compared on common purge-aware expanding out-of-sample folds.

Untouched holdout

A final temporal holdout remains outside model selection.

Execution gates

Automated live broker orders remain gated behind research validation and execution checks.
